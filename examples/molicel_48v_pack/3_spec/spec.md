## 5. Protection subset

**Modeled** (limits from DS, scaled in §4):
- Undervoltage cutoff: pack < 32.5 V → discharge terminated
- Overcurrent: |I_pack| > 180 A continuous → flag
- Temperature window check: input temperature outside −40…+60 °C (discharge) → flag

**Explicitly OUT OF SCOPE:** balancing, contactor/precharge logic, charger (CC-CV) behavior, cell-to-cell variation, thermal dynamics (→ candidate D), aging (TR cycle data not used).

## 6. Simulation scenario
